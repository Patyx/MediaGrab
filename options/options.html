MediaGrab Helper (Windows)

Checking…

The helper adds: the Windows Save dialog opening at your last folder, yt-dlp for YouTube and other sites MediaGrab doesn't support itself, and MP3 audio (converted with ffmpeg).

  1. In the extension folder, open helper and double-click install.bat. It downloads yt-dlp, ffmpeg and Deno (~200 MB) into %LOCALAPPDATA%\MediaGrab. No admin rights or winget needed.
  2. Come back here and click Check again.
