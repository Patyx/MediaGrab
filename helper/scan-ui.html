How it works

For every video: read resolution, codec and bitrate; skip files already efficiently encoded; pick 3–5 six-second windows across the video by its bitrate (calm and busy parts); encode them with your real HEVC settings; optionally score them with VMAF; and predict the compressed size. Results are cached, so rescans only analyse new or changed files. Encode time is estimated from your speed table (Settings).
